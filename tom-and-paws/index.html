How to play

Move: WASD or arrow keys. On a touchscreen: drag anywhere on the left half to move (a joystick appears under your thumb), tap the right side to build or throw, and tap the big buttons for powers!

Playing as Cat: don't get caught! Grab all fish to open the star exit door, or survive until the timer ends. Build to outsmart the dog: pick a block with 1–4, then click a tile near you (or press E to build in front of you).

Fence — blocks the dog (he has to chew it!) · Bush — hide inside, breaks your scent · Puddle — dogs slip and slow down · Squeaky Toy — dogs can't resist it!

Playing as Dog: catch that cat before time runs out! Follow the green scent trail. Run into fences and crates to chew through them.

Abilities: SPACE dash/sprint · Q puff cloud / bark · R super sniff (dog).

The journey: Forest → Iceberg Island → Candy World (beware the Candy Boss — it shoots candy out of its mouth, DODGE!) → buy the Shield to enter the Volcano → defeat the BOSS → unlock the MAX LEVEL, where the giant boss cat brings a whole minion army!

Eruptions: in the Volcano, watch the timer — when it hits zero, lava rocks rain down again and again! Dodge the red circles... or trick the dog into them.

Buy the Hidey Hole in the Wardrobe: place it (5), jump in, and pop out somewhere the dog will never guess!

Bonus worlds (Flower Island opens after the Candy Boss): pick flowers for points — but spiders crawl on them and pinch! In the FLOWER DUEL, collect every flower, then throw them (SPACE / click) at the Flower Monarch — 3 each, winner takes victory (+10 points!). In Waterline Cove the tide floods the map — water hides your scent but everyone swims slow. In Spider Grove, press F to Web-Zip toward your mouse!

In Sky Kingdom, wind gusts shove everyone across the map (the dog too — use it!) and bounce clouds launch you at super speed.

Energy: you have 3 hearts per level. Taxis and boss candy each knock one off — lose all 3 and it\u2019s restart time! (The dog only gets stunned by taxis... lucky mutt.)

In City Streets, taxis zoom down the roads and bonk anyone in the way — you AND the dog. Look both ways... or don\u2019t, and lure the dog into rush hour.

In Puppy Rescue, everything flips: the cat gang hunts YOU! Touch a lost puppy and it follows you in a little parade — walk it back to the doghouse to save it. Get pounced and you lose energy AND your newest follower runs off scared. Save all 5! (As the cat, you rescue kittens from guard dogs.)

Endless Chase: infinite waves of cats that get faster and meaner forever. Every 5th wave is a BOSS. How far can you get?

Doughnut Dash: you play the hungry dog! Eat every doughnut before the timer ends. Some hop, some roll, and the golden one is a BIG yum. Ovens pop out more. A sneaky kitty tries to steal them — Q woof so they drop it, SPACE zoomies, E SUPER CHOMP (doughnuts fly into your mouth!). Bounce clouds go BOING, and halfway through it’s Sprinkle Frenzy!

Zombie Kitty (6 levels): the kitties turned green and sleepy-silly, and they want a HUG. Don’t let them catch you! Grab every treat, then sprint to the glowing HOME door. Levels 1–3 teach the basics; 4–6 add moon-cheese bridges, doorbell freezes, and a straight-line walker. SPACE zoomies, Q woof, E moon howl. 3 hearts. Still silly — never scary!

Lost Sock Rescue: find every lost sock and carry it to the laundry basket before the raccoon steals it! Three cozy laundry levels.

Sticker Book: secrets and wins drop stickers. Fill a page for a coin cheer!

Daily Parade: once each real day, a short special on the menu. Missing a day does nothing bad.

Moon Fair (after Candy Boss): ride moon balloons (WHOOSH!), then the parade finale!

Dog upgrades: spend coins in the Wardrobe on permanent powers — faster paws, bigger bark, longer sprint, extra hearts. They last forever!

Twists: every level now rolls a random twist — cat swarms, fog, ice, wind, critters — so no two runs are the same.

Make a Level: paint your own map, then play it!

Score points for everything — chain pickups fast for COMBO multipliers, escape a close chase for a CLOSE CALL bonus, and beat your best score on every level!

Collect coins to buy skins and gear. Earn up to 3 stars per level!
